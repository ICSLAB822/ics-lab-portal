We are pleased to announce that our paper, titled **"Speak and Be Known: Authenticating Users via Ear Canal Deformation on Earbuds,"** has been accepted **with no further changes** as a **regular paper** in an upcoming issue of **IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (TMC)**.

This work explores a novel, earbud-native biometric authentication approach by leveraging **speech-induced ear canal deformation**, captured via an **embedded barometer**. Unlike traditional voice-based authentication that can be vulnerable to replay or synthetic voice spoofing, our method relies on **physiological motion patterns** reflected in in-ear pressure dynamics, enabling robust and passive user verification on everyday earbuds.

**Key contributions of this work include:**
1. A practical earbud-based authentication framework that uses barometric sensing to capture user-specific in-ear pressure dynamics during speech.
2. A deployable end-to-end system design with extensive experimental validation, including ablations and evaluations under diverse environmental and adversarial conditions.
3. Demonstrated robustness against traditional voice spoofing threats by authenticating through physiological deformation signatures rather than acoustic content.

The final accepted version will appear in a future issue of IEEE TMC, and the accepted version may be posted on IEEE Xplore under the journal’s preprint model after final materials are submitted.
