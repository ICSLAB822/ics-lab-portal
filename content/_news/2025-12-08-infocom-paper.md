We are pleased to announce that our paper, **"Baro2Talk: Reconstructing Spectrograms from Ear Canal Pressure for Voice-free Communication,"** has been accepted by **IEEE INFOCOM 2026**.

Baro2Talk is a silent-speech interface that captures pressure variations caused by silent articulation through miniature barometers embedded in standard earbuds. It reconstructs Mel spectrograms from these signals and uses a pretrained automatic speech-recognition model to decode them into text, avoiding the privacy leakage and environmental sensitivity of acoustic input.

The prototype achieves a 6.5% character error rate, a 9.9% word error rate, and a 0.081 spectral convergence score under silent, mobile, and noisy conditions.
