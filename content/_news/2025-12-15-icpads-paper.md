We are pleased to announce that two ICS LAB papers were accepted by **IEEE ICPADS 2025**, held in Hefei, China, from December 14–17, 2025:

1. **"AURORA: Adaptive Audio-Video Multi-Scale Attention Fusion for Deepfake Detection"** introduces a cross-level multimodal fusion framework that combines audio and visual forgery cues. On FakeAVCeleb, AURORA reaches 94.32% accuracy and a 93.66% AUC. [Publication record and PDF]({{ '/publications/icpads2025-AURORA/' | relative_url }})
2. **"Aclipse: Attention-based Cascaded Learning Enabling Privacy-preserving Speech Emotion Recognition"** protects speech content with targeted adversarial perturbations while retaining information needed for emotion recognition. [Publication record and PDF]({{ '/publications/icpads2025-aclipse/' | relative_url }})

Congratulations to all authors and collaborators.
